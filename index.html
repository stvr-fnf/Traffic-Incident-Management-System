    Response Team Allocation Matrix

    Accident
    Dispatch Police / Ambulance immediately for rescue and clearance.
    Vehicle Breakdown
    Assign Heavy Recovery / Towing Team to clear stalled vehicle.
    Traffic Congestion
    Deploy Traffic Control Wardens for manual signal override.
    Road Closure
    Coordinate with Road Maintenance for barricades and diversion signs.

    Incident Status Workflow

    1. Reported: Logged into control desk queue.
    2. Assigned: Response unit alerted and dispatched.
    3. In Progress: Unit on site actively clearing road.
    4. Resolved: Traffic flow restored; archived in history.
